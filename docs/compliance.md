# Compliance notes

- **Health data is personal data (India, DPDP Act 2023).** Consent per purpose on screen 02;
  deletion on request ("Delete my data"); minimal retention (files never stored, claim data 7 days).
  DPDP duties are enforceable from 13 May 2027; the app follows them now anyway.
- **IRDAI:** ClaimSense is a self-help estimate tool. It never files claims, sells or recommends
  policies, or takes fees. Labels never say "approved"; every result carries the disclaimer
  "Estimate, not a guarantee. Only your insurer decides. Not medical or legal advice."
- **Gemini free tier:** Google may use free-tier content to improve its products, and human reviewers
  may read it. So the demo accepts **synthetic documents only**, the consent screen says so, and a
  real launch would need Gemini's paid tier.
- **Accepted risk:** sign-up with a taken email returns `EMAIL_TAKEN`, revealing that an account exists.
